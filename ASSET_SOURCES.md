# Caliceti assets

The logo and restaurant/history/event photography in `public/images/` were copied from the public Caliceti site and optimized locally as WebP files. The food photography was replaced for this redesign with a cohesive set of generated, commercial-style food images, optimized as JPEGs. Generated dish imagery is illustrative; it does not claim to be a photograph of a specific served plate.

Food image replacements: `food-hero-v2.jpg`, `food-news-v2.jpg`, `food-weekly-tortelloni-v2.jpg`, `food-menu-v2.jpg`, `food-gourmet-v2.jpg`, `food-delivery-v2.jpg`, and the four `food-product-*-v2.jpg` catalog images.
